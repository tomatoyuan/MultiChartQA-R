The article, titled “U.S. has changed in key ways in the past decade, from tech use to demographics,” by Pew Research Center, details the significant changes in the United States over the past decade. Technologically, tech use including smartphones and social media has become widespread, with social media now a major news source. Demographically, Millennials have become the largest generation in the labor force, and nonwhites are the majority among newborns and K - 12 public school students. In the labor market, the unemployment rate has dropped to a near - record low since the Great Recession, but the employment rate has not fully recovered. Household size is likely to increase for the first time in at least 160 years. There have also been shifts in religious practices, with fewer Americans attending church regularly and more identifying as non - religious. Additionally, public opinion has changed, with growing support for marijuana legalization and same - sex marriage.
