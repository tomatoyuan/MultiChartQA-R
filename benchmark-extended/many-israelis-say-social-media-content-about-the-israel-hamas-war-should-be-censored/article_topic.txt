The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 1,001 Israeli adults from March 3 to April 4, 2024, explores Israelis' social - media use and views on censoring war - related content. It shows that while about 74% of Israelis use social media, only 22% often or sometimes post about political and social issues, and just 18% regularly post about the Israel - Hamas war. Many Israelis think certain social - media content related to the war should be censored, such as posts calling for violence (92%), expressing support for Hamas (87%), showing graphic violence (72%), and expressing sympathy for Gazan civilians (59%). Jewish and Arab Israelis, as well as those on different ideological sides, have varying views on censorship. For instance, Jewish Israelis are more likely to support censoring content than Arab Israelis, and those on the right are more in favor of censorship than those on the left, especially regarding posts sympathetic to Gazan civilians and critical of the government.
