This article presents key facts about the U.S. Black population ahead of Black History Month. The Black population reached 48.3 million in 2023, up 33% since 2000, with increases in multi - racial and Hispanic Black subgroups, reflecting a broader national multi - racial shift and the arrival of new immigrants. It has grown fastest in states like Utah, Arizona, Nevada, and Minnesota, while some states like Mississippi, Illinois, and D.C. saw declines. The New York City metro area had the most Black residents in 2023, and Atlanta had the highest percentage of Black residents among large metro areas. The Black population is relatively young, with a median age of 32.6 years in 2023. Educational attainment is rising, with more Black adults having at least a bachelor’s degree, especially Black women. Black Americans are less likely to be married than non - Black Americans, and about 18% of married Black adults have non - Black spouses. Median annual income in 2023 was $54,000 for Black households, varying by subgroup.
