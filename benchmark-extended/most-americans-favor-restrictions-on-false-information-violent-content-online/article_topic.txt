A Pew Research Center survey from June 5 - 11, 2023, of 5,115 U.S. adults reveals that most Americans believe both the U.S. government and technology companies should take steps to restrict false information and extremely violent content online, with more support for tech - company moderation. Support for such restrictions has grown in recent years; for example, the share of those favoring the federal government restricting false information rose from 39% in 2018 to 55% in 2023. There are partisan and age differences: Democrats are more supportive than Republicans of both government and tech - company restrictions on false and violent content, and the partisan gap on false - information restrictions has grown since 2018. Older Americans (ages 50 and up) are more likely than younger adults to support these restrictions, though younger adults' support has also increased substantially since 2018.
