The article explores the impact of the coronavirus pandemic on mental health in the United States. It presents findings from Pew Research Center and other organizations, indicating that at least 41% of U.S. adults experienced high - level psychological distress during the pandemic, with young adults, women, lower - income individuals, and those with disabilities more affected. High school students also faced significant mental health challenges, with girls, LGB students reporting more negative experiences. U.S. parents are highly worried about their children's mental health, with concerns about anxiety and depression topping the list, and many parents of teenagers are worried about the impact of social media. Looking back, K - 12 parents believe the first year of the pandemic had a negative impact on their children's emotional well - being and education, especially white parents and those from upper - income households.
