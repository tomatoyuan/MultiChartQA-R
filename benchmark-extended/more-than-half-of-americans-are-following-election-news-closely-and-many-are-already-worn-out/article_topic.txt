This article, based on a Pew Research Center survey conducted from April 8 - 14, 2024, explores Americans' habits and attitudes towards 2024 U.S. presidential election news. Over half (58%) are following candidate news closely, a bit higher than in April 2020. Republicans and older adults are more likely to follow closely. About 62% are worn - out by the coverage, similar to past elections, and those closely following are less fatigued. Republicans are slightly less likely to feel worn - out. Most Americans (57%) come across political news rather than actively seek it, with a large age gap; those closely following the election are more likely to seek it. A majority (62%) get election news from journalists and news organizations, and there are differences in news sources by age and political party, with older Americans and Democrats more likely to rely on news organizations.
