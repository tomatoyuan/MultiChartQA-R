This article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 9,609 U.S. adults conducted from Nov. 12 - 17, 2024, explores how Americans plan to celebrate Thanksgiving. Around 91% of Americans celebrate the holiday, with variations based on age and immigration status. Most (74%) plan to have Thanksgiving dinner with others, and many aspects of the celebration, such as the dinner location, time, and activities, vary by age, region, and religious group. Common traditions include saying grace or expressing gratitude, and driving is the main mode of travel for those dining away from home. Beyond eating, Americans engage in a wide - range of activities like watching sports or parades, and conversation topics may include work, school, the recent presidential election, and pop culture. There are also differences in conversation likelihood based on election voting choices and political ideology.
