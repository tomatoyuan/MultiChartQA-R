This article presents key facts about the wealth of immigrant households in the US during the COVID - 19 pandemic, using data from the Census Bureau’s Survey of Income and Program Participation. It shows that while the median wealth of immigrant households increased by 42% from December 2019 to December 2021 (compared to 29% for U.S.-born households), they still lagged far behind in overall wealth in 2021. Wealth varies by factors such as Hispanic or non - Hispanic status, immigrant generation, years of arrival in the US, citizenship, English proficiency, and region of origin. Longer - term immigrants generally have more wealth, and U.S. citizens and English - proficient immigrants tend to be wealthier. European and Asian immigrant households typically have higher wealth than those from the Americas or Caribbean. Home equity makes up a larger share of immigrant household wealth, and immigrant households with physical assets have a larger share of wealth in them, while those with financial assets have a smaller share compared to U.S. - born households.
