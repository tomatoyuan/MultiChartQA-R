This article, sourced from Pew Research Center, provides comprehensive insights into the U.S. immigrant population as of 2022 and 2023. It notes that the U.S. has more immigrants than any other country, with the foreign - born population reaching a record 47.8 million in 2023. The origin countries of immigrants have changed significantly since 1965, from a historical dominance of European immigrants to a current situation where about half come from Latin America and a quarter from Asia. Most immigrants (77%) are in the country legally, but the unauthorized immigrant population has grown since 2022. Many unauthorized immigrants have temporary legal protections. Immigrants are concentrated in states like California, Texas, Florida, and New York, and in major metropolitan areas. Over 30 million immigrants are in the U.S. workforce, with lawful immigrants making up the majority. On average, immigrants have lower education levels than the U.S. - born population, but educational attainment varies by origin. About half of immigrants aged 5 and older are proficient English speakers, and Spanish is the most commonly spoken language at home among immigrants.
