This article, a Pew Research Center analysis from the Pew - Knight Initiative, examines how news influencers (individuals with at least 100,000 followers on major social media platforms who regularly post about current events) talked about Donald Trump and Kamala Harris during the 2024 presidential election. It finds that most influencers mentioned both candidates, but more posts were about Trump. Four - in - ten influencers were more critical than supportive of each candidate, with posts about Harris being more negative. Right - leaning influencers posted more overall, and a majority of posts about the candidates were on X. Left - and right - leaning influencers had different stances on the candidates, with left - leaners supporting Harris and right - leaners supporting Trump. News influencers with newsroom connections were more critical of Trump, while those without were more critical of Harris. After the election, influencers became less critical of both candidates and more supportive of the new president, with changes in support and criticism levels varying by political leaning.
