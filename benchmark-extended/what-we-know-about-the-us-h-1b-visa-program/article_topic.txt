This article delves into the U.S. H - 1B visa program, which enables employers to temporarily hire foreign workers in specialized occupations. It has divided Republicans, with tech leaders like Elon Musk supporting it while others question its impact on American workers. Bipartisan calls for reforms seek more oversight. The number of approved H - 1B applications has climbed in recent years, reaching nearly 400,000 in 2024, with most being renewals. Denial rates spiked under Trump and fell under Biden. Key facts include that India is the top birth - country for H - 1B workers, the share of those with a master's degree has increased, computer - related jobs are the most common occupation, Amazon has been the top employer since 2020, New York had the most approvals in 2023, and College Station, Texas, has the highest concentration of approvals. Additionally, about 40% of U.S. adults think high - skilled workers should be a top priority for legal immigration, and 60% of voters believe legal immigrants fill jobs U.S. citizens don't want.
