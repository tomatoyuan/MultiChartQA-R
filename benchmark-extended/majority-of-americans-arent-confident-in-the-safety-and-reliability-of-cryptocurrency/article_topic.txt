The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey from Feb. 7 - 11, 2024, discusses Americans' views and experiences with cryptocurrency. Cryptocurrency markets face challenges like value drops, lawsuits, and potential regulations. About 63% of Americans have little to no confidence in its reliability and safety, and this view has remained relatively stable since 2023. Older adults and those not invested in it are more skeptical. Overall, 17% of U.S. adults have used cryptocurrency, with no significant growth since 2021; men under 50, Asian adults, upper - income households are more likely to use it, and use is similar among Republicans and Democrats. A majority of past users still hold it, though more are divesting, especially upper - income users. Many users report their investments have done worse or as expected, and in 2023, most said it hadn't affected their finances. In 2023, about three - quarters of users first used it one to five years earlier, with Black users and those from lower - income households more likely to have started in the previous year.
