The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey conducted from Sept. 30 - Oct. 6, 2024, examines Americans' views on cellphone bans for middle and high school students. Most (68%) U.S. adults support a ban on students using cellphones during class, but only about one - third (36%) back a full - day ban. Younger adults are less supportive of both types of bans compared to older age groups. There are few differences in views between K - 12 parents and non - parents, except on issues like bullying and a parent's ability to reach their child. Republicans are more likely than Democrats to support both classroom and full - day bans. Those who support classroom bans mainly cite reducing distractions, while opponents largely say parents need to be able to reach their children.
