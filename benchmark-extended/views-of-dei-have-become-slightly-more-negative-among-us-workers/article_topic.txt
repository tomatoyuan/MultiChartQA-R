This Pew Research Center article reveals that U.S. workers' views on diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) efforts in the workplace have become more negative since 2023. In 2024, the share of workers seeing DEI focus as a good thing dropped from 56% to 52%, while those viewing it as bad rose from 16% to 21%. Republicans, men, and Asian workers have notably shifted to more negative stances. Additionally, more workers now say their companies pay too much attention to DEI (19% in 2024 vs. 14% in 2023). In a separate survey of U.S. adults, more believe DEI helps Black, Hispanic, and Asian men and women, but far more think it hurts White men (36% vs. 14%). Views vary by gender, race, ethnicity, and political party, with Republicans more likely to see DEI as harmful across different groups.
