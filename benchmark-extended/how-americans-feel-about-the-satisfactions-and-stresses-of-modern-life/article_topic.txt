The article, based on multiple Pew Research Center surveys conducted between 2015 and 2018, explores how Americans are coping with modern - life satisfactions and stresses. Most Americans find some meaning in their jobs, and those who do tend to be more satisfied with life. While many feel busy, few say it harms their health, though parents, especially those with young children, struggle more with the pace of life. Technology has a mixed impact on workers, and information overload can cause stress. Social media is more likely to provide connections than negative experiences, but some still encounter depressing or lonely content. Teens view anxiety and depression as major peer problems and face academic pressure. A majority are somewhat satisfied with their social life, yet many see room for improvement in their support networks. Higher - income and white Americans are more likely to find meaning through friends, and belonging to a community ranks low as a source of meaning.
