The article discusses who pays for local news in the U.S. despite local newsrooms facing financial struggles with declining revenue and staffing, and the public's lack of awareness of these challenges. A Pew Research Center survey from last fall shows that few Americans pay for local news, but older Americans, white adults, those with college degrees, those who prefer print news, and those more civically engaged or attached to their community are more likely to do so. Print news consumers and more civically - engaged individuals are also more pessimistic about the financial well - being of local news media. While those interested in many local news topics aren't more likely to pay (except those who find no topics important), they are more likely to think local news media are doing well financially.
