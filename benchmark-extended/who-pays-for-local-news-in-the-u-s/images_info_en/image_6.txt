1. Among U.S. adults who say 5 or more local news topics are important for daily life (23% of the public), the proportion who have paid for local news in the past year is 15%.
2. Among U.S. adults who say 5 or more local news topics are important for daily life (23% of the public), the proportion who say their local news outlets are doing well financially is 79%.
3. Among U.S. adults who say 3 - 4 local news topics are important for daily life (28% of the public), the proportion who have paid for local news in the past year is 16%.
4. Among U.S. adults who say 3 - 4 local news topics are important for daily life (28% of the public), the proportion who say their local news outlets are doing well financially is 77%.
5. Among U.S. adults who say 1 - 2 local news topics are important for daily life (33% of the public), the proportion who have paid for local news in the past year is 15%.
6. Among U.S. adults who say 1 - 2 local news topics are important for daily life (33% of the public), the proportion who say their local news outlets are doing well financially is 69%.
7. Among U.S. adults who say no local news topics are important for daily life (16% of the public), the proportion who have paid for local news in the past year is 9%.
8. Among U.S. adults who say no local news topics are important for daily life (16% of the public), the proportion who say their local news outlets are doing well financially is 55%.
9. The survey was conducted from Oct. 15 - Nov. 8, 2018.
