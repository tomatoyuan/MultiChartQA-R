This article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 8,842 U.S. adults from Sept. 25 to Oct. 1, 2023, reveals that only about one - third of Americans think climate scientists understand very well if climate change is happening, and even fewer believe they understand well its effects on extreme weather, causes, and solutions. Americans' ratings of climate scientists' understanding are slightly lower than two years ago and the same or lower than in 2016. There are significant partisan differences, with Democrats rating scientists' understanding much higher than Republicans. Education level also affects Democrats' ratings but not Republicans'. Regarding scientists' influence on policy, half of Americans say they have too little influence, a 4 - point drop from 2021, and there are deep partisan and ideological divides on this issue.
