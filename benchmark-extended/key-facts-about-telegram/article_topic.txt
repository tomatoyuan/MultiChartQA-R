This article from Pew Research Center presents key facts about Telegram, an alternative social - media platform. Telegram has been useful for journalists and civilians in conflict zones but has faced controversy regarding content removal. The study used a multi - method approach including surveys, site audits, account and post content analysis. Only 27% of U.S. adults have heard of Telegram, and just 2% use it for news, far less than established platforms like Facebook and YouTube. Telegram supports free speech but doesn't claim to oppose censorship or be an alternative to Big Tech. Few prominent Telegram accounts are right - leaning or pro - Trump. Many regular news consumers on Telegram have positive experiences, with a large share satisfied, expecting accurate news, and finding it helpful for understanding events. In June 2022, significant shares of prominent accounts posted about guns, LGBTQ issues, vaccines, etc. A majority of news consumers from the seven alternative platforms, including Telegram, identify as Republican or lean Republican, unlike those using established platforms.
