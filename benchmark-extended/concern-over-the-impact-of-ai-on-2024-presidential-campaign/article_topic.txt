The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 9,720 U.S. adults from Aug. 26 - Sept. 2, 2024, explores Americans' concerns about the impact of AI on the 2024 presidential campaign. A majority of Americans are worried that AI will be used to create and distribute fake or misleading information about candidates and campaigns, with 39% believing it will be used mostly for bad purposes and only 5% for good. While 77% think major tech companies like Facebook, X, TikTok, and Google have a responsibility to prevent platform misuse for election - influencing, only 20% are confident they will do so. Public confidence in these companies has declined since 2018. Views on tech companies' responsibility and confidence in them vary by party, with Democrats more likely to think companies are responsible. Republicans and Democrats have similar views on AI's influence on the election, but views differ by age, with younger adults more likely to say AI will be used equally for good and bad and older adults more worried about AI's influence.
