This article, based on recent Pew Research Center studies, presents eight key facts about Americans and TikTok. It reveals that public support for a TikTok ban has declined, with Republicans more likely to favor it than Democrats. A third of U.S. adults use TikTok, with younger adults being more frequent users, and its user - base has grown rapidly. Almost all users go for entertainment. Around six - in - ten teens use the platform, with girls, Black, and Hispanic teens being more engaged. TikTok users have mixed views on its impact on democracy. About half of adult users get news on TikTok, and news consumers are more likely to be younger, female, and Democratic. TikTok's news influencers differ from those on other sites in terms of gender, political orientation, and LGBTQ+ support. Many users see breaking news, but other content types are more common. A small share of users produce most of the content, and about half of adult users have posted videos, with posting behavior not varying much by age.
