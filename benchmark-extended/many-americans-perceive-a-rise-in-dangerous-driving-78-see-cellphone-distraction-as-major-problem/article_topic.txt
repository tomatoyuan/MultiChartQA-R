As millions of Americans gear up for holiday travel, a Pew Research Center survey reveals that many U.S. adults feel driving in their area has become more dangerous since before the coronavirus pandemic. 49% say people in their community are driving less safely than five years ago, while only 9% perceive an improvement. Those who drive at least once a week and those in suburban areas are more likely to notice the decline in driving safety. Cellphone distraction tops the list of concerns, with 78% seeing it as a major problem, followed by speeding and aggressive driving. Views on dangerous driving behaviors vary by community type, and around a quarter of Americans say they frequently see road - rage, with urban dwellers being the most likely to report it.
