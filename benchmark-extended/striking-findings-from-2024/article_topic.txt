This article presents 14 striking research findings from 2024 gathered by the Pew Research Center, covering various pivotal news stories. It includes projections about the U.S. centenarian population, which is expected to more than quadruple in the next 30 years with changes in gender, racial, and ethnic compositions; shifts in Republican and Democratic confidence in U.S. vote - counts after the 2024 election; views on immigrants filling jobs Americans don't want; young adults getting news from social - media influencers; global and U.S. perceptions of U.S. democracy; attitudes towards the 2024 - 25 COVID - 19 vaccine; generational differences in sympathies towards Palestinians and Israelis and support for U.S. military aid to Israel; concerns of public K - 12 teachers about school shootings; changes in voters' perceptions of Biden and Trump's mental sharpness; increased perceptions of discrimination against Jews and Muslims; partisan differences in views of X's impact on democracy; "link rot" on the internet; the record - breaking age gap between Trump and his vice - president JD Vance; and Americans' skepticism about the cost and value of a college degree.
