The article presents key facts about the 3.8 million U.S. public school teachers. It notes that in 2024, teachers have been in the spotlight, with the Democratic vice - presidential nominee highlighting his teaching career and a congressional hearing focusing on teacher - related “crises”. Demographically, most K - 12 teachers are women, and the teaching force is younger and less racially/ethnically diverse than the student population. Teachers have relatively low job satisfaction, especially regarding pay, training, and benefits, and many are likely to look for new jobs. Most describe their job as stressful and overwhelming, which may be related to understaffing. Newer teachers are more likely to find the job fulfilling and enjoyable. There is a growing pipeline problem as fewer people are completing teacher - related educational requirements. Teachers are split on recommending the profession, and they rate students' academic performance and behavior relatively low. They also face various classroom challenges, and about half want the public to know that teaching is a difficult job.
