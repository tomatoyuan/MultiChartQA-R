The article presents how the makeup of the 119th Congress, which convened on January 3, 2025, has changed over time through seven charts. It notes that this Congress is the most racially and ethnically diverse in history, with 139 non - White members, and it includes the nation's first openly transgender legislator. Women make up 28% of voting members, and the number of openly LGBTQ members has increased. The Congress is slightly younger, with Gen Xers outnumbering Baby Boomers in the House. The number of veterans has slightly increased but remains far lower than in past decades, and the share of immigrants has ticked up but is below historical highs. While most members are Christian, their numbers have declined, and the religious makeup of Congress shows a variety of other faiths and unaffiliated members, though in different proportions compared to the U.S. population.
