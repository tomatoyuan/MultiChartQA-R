The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 1,391 U.S. teens aged 13 - 17 from Sept. 18 to Oct. 10, 2024, examines American teens' use and understanding of ChatGPT in a school setting. It reveals that the share of teens using ChatGPT for schoolwork has doubled from 13% in 2023 to 26% in 2024, with Black and Hispanic teens and those in 11th and 12th grade being more likely users. Teens are more supportive of using ChatGPT for research (54% find it acceptable) than for math problems (29%) or writing essays (18%). Awareness of ChatGPT has grown from 67% in 2023 to 79% in 2024, and those more familiar with it are more likely to use it for schoolwork and consider it acceptable; however, awareness varies by race/ethnicity and household income, though the lowest - income households had the greatest increase in awareness over the past year.
