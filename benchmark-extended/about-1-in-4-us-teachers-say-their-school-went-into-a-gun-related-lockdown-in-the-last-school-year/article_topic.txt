This Pew Research Center article examines U.S. public K - 12 teachers' views on school shootings, their preparedness, and prevention strategies. In 2023, 59% of teachers are at least somewhat worried about a shooting at their school, with 23% experiencing a gun - related lockdown in the 2022 - 23 school year, more common among high - school and urban teachers. About 39% feel their school did a fair or poor job preparing them for an active shooter, and this perception varies by school location and presence of security. Teachers think better mental health treatment is most effective for prevention, followed by police/armed security and metal detectors, while allowing teachers to carry guns is seen as least effective. Teachers' and parents' views on prevention strategies also differ by political party.
