This article, based on a Pew Research Center survey in eight Western European countries in late 2017, explores the differences between 18 - to 29 - year - olds and older age groups. Younger Europeans are more left - leaning, though not more likely to hold populist views, and have mixed evaluations of traditional center - left parties, often preferring left - leaning, green and progressive parties. They are more socially progressive on issues like gay rights and women in the workplace, more positive about immigration's economic, cultural and security impacts, and somewhat more supportive of the EU in terms of its economic benefits and power transfer from national governments.
