This article from Pew Research Center presents key findings about online dating in the U.S. based on a survey of 6,034 adults in July 2022. It covers various aspects such as user demographics (younger and LGB adults, men, those with some college education, and unmarried adults are more likely to use online dating), popular dating platforms (Tinder is most popular, especially among under - 30s), success in finding partners (10% of partnered adults met through online dating, more common among under - 30s and LGB), user experiences (divided between positive and negative, with differences by gender and sexual orientation), payment for platforms (35% have paid, varying by income, age, and gender), reasons for using (meeting long - term partners and casual dating are top reasons, with men more likely to cite casual sex), public views on online dating (42% think it makes finding a partner easier, 43% think there are the right amount of options), skepticism about dating algorithms, divided opinions on safety (48% think it's safe, with differences by age and usage), support for background checks (60% in favor, less support among users), and prevalence of unwanted behaviors and scams (more common among younger women and men under 50).
