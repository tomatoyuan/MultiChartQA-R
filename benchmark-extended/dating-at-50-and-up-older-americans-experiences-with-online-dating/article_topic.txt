The article, “Dating at 50 and up: Older Americans’ experiences with online dating,” presents findings from a Pew Research Center survey on how Americans aged 50 and older experience online dating. It reveals that 17% of them have ever used a dating site or app, with usage varying by age bracket, and only 3% currently use or used in the last year. Different age groups also prefer different dating platforms, like older users being more likely to use Match and less likely to use Tinder. Older adults date online for reasons such as finding a long - term partner or dating casually, similar to younger users. Older women are more likely to have negative online dating experiences, while older men are more likely to pay for dating sites. About 47% of older online daters have encountered scammers, and over 45% have experienced at least one unwanted behavior on dating platforms, with differences based on age and gender.
