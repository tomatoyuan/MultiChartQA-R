This article from Pew Research Center discusses the shift in Americans' views about the media's influence over a year. In 2021, more Americans (41%) say news organizations are growing in influence compared to those who say their influence is declining (33%), a contrast to 2020 when nearly half (48%) thought the media's influence was waning. The 2021 figures are similar to 2011. This shift occurred among both Republicans and Democrats, though Republicans are still more likely than Democrats to say the media are losing standing. Trust in the media is closely related to views on its influence, with those more trusting seeing it as growing and those less trusting seeing it as waning. Additionally, Black and Hispanic Americans are more likely to think the media's influence is growing, while white Americans are evenly split, and women are more likely than men to say news organizations' influence is growing.
