This Pew Research Center article focuses on Americans' views regarding a potential TikTok ban and its perceived threat to national security. The survey of 5,123 adults in February - March 2025 shows that support for a TikTok ban has declined from 50% in March 2023 to 34% now, while opposition has risen from 22% to 32%. Republicans are more likely to support a ban than Democrats, and non - TikTok users are more likely to back it than users. Those who support a ban mainly cite data security and Chinese ownership as major reasons, with partisan and user - related differences in other reasons. Opponents are primarily concerned about free - speech restrictions, and TikTok users are more likely to oppose the ban due to information/entertainment, lack of evidence of threat, and impact on income. The share of Americans seeing TikTok as a national security threat has also dropped from 59% in 2023 to 49% now, with a decline in the view of it as a major threat.
