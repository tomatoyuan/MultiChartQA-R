The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey from Aug. 31 - Sept. 7, 2020, explores how Americans get news in the digital age. It reveals that over eight - in - ten U.S. adults get news from digital devices, more than those using TV, radio, or print. Around half prefer digital platforms for news, with news websites or apps being the most preferred among them. There are significant age - related differences in news consumption: those 50 and older use both TV and digital devices, while younger age groups (18 - 49) predominantly rely on digital devices. Among 18 - 29 - year - olds, social media is the most common digital news source, whereas most other age groups turn to news websites more often. The survey also mentions changes in measuring news consumption to improve data collection.
