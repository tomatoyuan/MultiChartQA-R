1. Among U.S. adults aged 18 - 29, the proportion who say it is at least somewhat important that the news they get comes from journalists or reporters around the same age as them is 23%.
2. Among U.S. adults aged 30 - 49, the proportion who say it is at least somewhat important that the news they get comes from journalists or reporters around the same age as them is 16%.
3. Among U.S. adults aged 50 - 64, the proportion who say it is at least somewhat important that the news they get comes from journalists or reporters around the same age as them is 12%.
4. Among U.S. adults aged 65 and above, the proportion who say it is at least somewhat important that the news they get comes from journalists or reporters around the same age as them is 10%.
The survey period is from March 13th to 19th, 2023.
