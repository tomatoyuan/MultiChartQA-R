This Pew Research Center article focuses on how U.S. LGBTQ adults view the Trump administration's policies, U.S. institutions, and policies related to transgender people. Conducted in January 2025, the survey found that 78% of LGBTQ adults expect Trump's policies to negatively impact transgender people and 71% expect a negative impact on gay, lesbian, or bisexual individuals, with significant differences based on party affiliation. Most LGBTQ adults see the Republican Party and Supreme Court as unfriendly, especially to transgender people, while they view the Democratic Party as more friendly. Regarding policies, most support protecting trans people from discrimination and requiring insurance coverage for gender - transition care, but views are mixed on issues like trans athletes and medical care for minors. There are also differences in views based on sexual orientation, with transgender adults generally more supportive of pro - trans policies and more opposed to restrictive ones, and party differences are stark on all policy measures.
