This article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 9,201 adults in August 2024, delves into Americans' views on undocumented immigrants. While about three - quarters of U.S. adults are concerned about illegal immigration and a majority support mass deportations, most (64%) believe undocumented immigrants should have a way to stay legally if certain requirements are met, and 43% of deportation supporters also hold this view. Different demographic groups vary in their support, with younger adults, Hispanic, Black, Asian adults, and Harris supporters more likely to back a legal path. However, support for allowing them to stay has declined from 75% in 2020 to 64% currently. Among those favoring a legal stay, security background checks are the most popular requirement. Reasons for not allowing a legal stay include concerns about law - breaking, fairness to legal immigrants, and resource usage. The unauthorized immigrant population in the U.S. has grown from 2019 to 2022, and some with temporary protection could face deportation if policies change.
