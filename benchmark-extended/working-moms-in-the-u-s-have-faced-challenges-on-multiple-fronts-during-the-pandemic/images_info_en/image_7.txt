1. Among all employed adults currently working from home all or most of the time, the proportion of parents of children <18 who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 50%.
2. Among all employed adults currently working from home all or most of the time, the proportion of non - parents who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 20%.
3. Among men who telework, the proportion of parents of children <18 who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 48%.
4. Among men who telework, the proportion of non - parents who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 19%.
5. Among women who telework, the proportion of parents of children <18 who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 52%.
6. Among women who telework, the proportion of non - parents who say it has been very or somewhat difficult for them to get their work done without interruptions since the beginning of the coronavirus outbreak is 21%.
7. The survey period is from Oct. 13 - 19, 2020.
