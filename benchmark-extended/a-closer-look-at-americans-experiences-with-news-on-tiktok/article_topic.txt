This article delves into Americans' experiences with news on TikTok. About half of TikTok users (17% of all U.S. adults) regularly get news on the site, yet fewer than 1% of the accounts they follow are journalists or news outlets. TikTok users obtain news from diverse sources, often without seeking it. Many follow institutional news sources along with other accounts, and non - newsroom accounts also post news and have wide audiences. TikTok news consumers get news from influencers, celebrities, and unknown individuals as much as or more than from traditional media. News influencers are popular, with 37% of TikTok users getting news from them and 84% having no news - organization background. Most TikTok users visit for entertainment but still encounter news, which often appears as funny or opinionated posts. Users may get news from non - followed accounts via the algorithm. Younger Americans are more likely to get news from TikTok and news influencers and are more passive news consumers, while older age groups are more likely to actively seek political news.
