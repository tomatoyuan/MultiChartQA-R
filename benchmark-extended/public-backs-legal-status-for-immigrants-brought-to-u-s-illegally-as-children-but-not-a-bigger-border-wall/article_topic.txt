The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey from Jan. 10 - 15, reveals the American public's opinions on two key immigration - policy issues: granting permanent legal status to immigrants brought to the U.S. illegally as children and substantially expanding the U.S. - Mexico border wall. A large majority (74%) favors legal status for child immigrants, while 60% oppose the wall expansion. There are significant partisan differences, with Democrats largely in support of legal status and against the wall, and Republicans more divided. Among Republicans, there are ideological, demographic, age, gender, and educational divides in views on both policies. Additionally, opinions on the wall vary widely across demographic, religious, and educational groups, with more people overall opposing the wall expansion, but different levels of support and opposition within subgroups such as race, age, religion, and education.
