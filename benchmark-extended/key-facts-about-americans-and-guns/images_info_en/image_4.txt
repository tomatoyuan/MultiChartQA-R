1. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of gun - owners who say they feel safer owning a gun/having a gun in the home is 81%.
2. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of those who have a gun in the household but are not gun - owners who say they feel safer owning a gun/having a gun in the home is 57%.
3. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of gun - owners who say they enjoy owning a gun/having a gun in the home is 71%.
4. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of those who have a gun in the household but are not gun - owners who say they enjoy owning a gun/having a gun in the home is 31%.
5. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of gun - owners who say they worry about having a gun in the home is 12%.
6. Among U.S. adults, the proportion of those who have a gun in the household but are not gun - owners who say they worry about having a gun in the home is 27%.
The survey period is from June 5th to 11th, 2023.
