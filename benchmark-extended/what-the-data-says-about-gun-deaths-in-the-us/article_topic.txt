This article, based on data from the CDC, FBI and other sources, delves into gun - related deaths in the United States. It reveals that nearly 47,000 people died from gun - related injuries in 2023, with gun deaths dropping for the second consecutive year but still remaining high. It examines various aspects such as the breakdown of gun deaths into murders, suicides, law - enforcement - related, accidental, and undetermined cases; the proportion of gun - involved murders and suicides among all such events; the changes in the number and rate of gun deaths over time; the differences in gun death rates across states; a comparison of the U.S. gun death rate with other countries; the number of fatalities in mass shootings (with challenges due to lack of a unified definition); the change in the number of mass shootings; and the types of firearms commonly used in gun murders in the U.S.
