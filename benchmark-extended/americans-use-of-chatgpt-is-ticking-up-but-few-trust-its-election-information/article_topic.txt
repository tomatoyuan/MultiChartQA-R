The article presents findings from a Pew Research Center survey on Americans' use of ChatGPT and their attitudes towards it. Since ChatGPT's public debut over a year ago, its usage among U.S. adults has increased to 23% in February 2024 from 18% in July 2023. Younger and highly - educated adults are more likely to use it. Employment use has risen from 8% in March 2023 to 20% in February 2024, and around one - in - five adults use it for learning or entertainment. When it comes to the 2024 U.S. presidential election, about four - in - ten Americans don't trust the election information from ChatGPT, and only 2% have a great deal or quite a bit of trust. Very few Americans (2%) have used the chatbot to find election - related information. Amid national attention on chatbots and misinformation, tech companies have pledged to prevent AI misuse in the election, but chatbots may still give misleading answers.
