This article from Pew Research Center explores U.S. adults' views on the emphasis given to certain aspects when raising boys and girls. It reveals differences in responses regarding each gender, such as more adults saying there's too little emphasis on encouraging boys to talk about their feelings and do well in school compared to girls. There are also differences based on factors like having minor - children, gender, and party affiliation. For instance, those without minor children are more likely to say there's too little emphasis on boys talking about feelings. Views have changed since 2017, with fewer people now saying there's too little focus on encouraging girls to do well in school, be leaders, or stand up for themselves. Gender - based differences show women more likely to advocate for boys talking about feelings, while men are more likely to emphasize boys standing up for themselves, being leaders, and doing well in school. Party - based differences are also significant, with Republicans more likely to stress boys standing up for themselves and being leaders, and Democrats more likely to emphasize the same for girls and both genders talking about feelings.
