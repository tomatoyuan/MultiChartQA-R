The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 1,316 U.S. parents of 13 - 17 - year - old teens conducted from April 14 to May 4, 2022, explores parents' concerns and practices regarding their teens' social media use. Key concerns include access to explicit content (46% extremely or very worried), time - wasting (42%), and distraction from homework (38%). Parental concerns vary by race/ethnicity (Hispanic parents more worried about many issues) and the teen's gender (parents of girls more concerned about mental - health impacts). A majority of parents monitor their teens' social media (57%) and some set screen - time limits (49%), with differences also seen by race, gender, and age of the teen. Additionally, 77% of parents believe teens today face a completely different set of issues compared to when they were teenagers.
