The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey from July 15 - Aug. 4, 2024, reveals that public support for a U.S. TikTok ban continues to decline, with the share of supporters dropping to 32% from 38% in fall 2023 and 50% in March 2023, while opposition has risen to 28% from 22% in March 2023 and uncertainty to 39% from 28%. TikTok's fate in the U.S. remains uncertain as a law requiring ByteDance to sell it or face a ban is being challenged in court. The article also examines views by political party (Republicans more likely to support a ban but support has dropped in both parties), TikTok use (users more likely to oppose a ban and support has decreased in both user and non - user groups), and likelihood of a ban (half of Americans think it's unlikely, with similar views across parties, users/non - users, and those who support/oppose the ban).
