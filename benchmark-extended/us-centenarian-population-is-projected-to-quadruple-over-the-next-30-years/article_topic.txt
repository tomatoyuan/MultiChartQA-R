This article discusses the projected growth of the U.S. centenarian population, which is expected to more than quadruple from an estimated 101,000 in 2024 to about 422,000 in 2054, rising from 0.03% to 0.1% of the overall population. The number of centenarians has steadily increased since 1950 and nearly tripled in the last three decades. Currently, women and White adults make up the majority, though their shares are projected to decrease. The overall U.S. population is also expected to age as life expectancy rises and birth rates decline. Globally, there are an estimated 722,000 centenarians in 2024, with Japan having the most, followed by the U.S., China, India, and Thailand. By 2054, the global centenarian population is projected to reach nearly 4 million, with China expected to have the largest number.
