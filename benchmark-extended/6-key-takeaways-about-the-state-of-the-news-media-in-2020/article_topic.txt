The article presents key takeaways about the state of the U.S. news media in 2020 from Pew Research Center's updated fact - sheets. It details various trends: newspapers earned more from circulation than advertising for the first time due to long - term ad revenue decline; cable news channels had explosive audience growth in 2020, with other TV news sectors seeing smaller increases; local TV stations had dramatically higher political ad revenue; individual giving became a larger part of public broadcasters' revenue; total advertising revenue is mostly digital, driven by mobile advertising growth; the audience for online audio like NPR podcasts grew, while terrestrial radio listenership declined in 2020, likely due to decreased automobile use during the pandemic.
