This article examines U.S. public opinion on key policy issues ahead of President Donald Trump's March 4, 2025 address to a joint session of Congress. It draws on Pew Research Center surveys, including one from Jan. 27 - Feb. 2, 2025. Economic issues are major perceived problems, with negative public assessments, partisan differences in views and predictions, and concerns about affordability. There are also stark partisan divides on immigration policy, with broad GOP support for Trump's immigration agenda and different views on birth - right citizenship. On Ukraine aid, views on NATO, confidence in federal civil servants, and issues related to trans people, opinions vary significantly by party. Additionally, climate change is a much bigger concern for Democrats than Republicans, with different priorities in climate policy.
