The article delves into what Trump supporters believe and expect following his decisive victory in the Nov. 5, 2024 presidential election. Drawing on Pew Research Center's pre - election surveys, it shows that most supporters saw Trump as a change candidate who would improve Washington. The economy and immigration were top issues for them, with immigration becoming more important compared to 2020, while issues like climate change and racial inequality were less significant. Trump supporters were unified on cultural issues such as biological sex, gun ownership, and the criminal justice system. They wanted a smaller government but opposed cuts to Social Security. They had negative views of national conditions and were highly concerned about prices. Most felt they understood Trump's policies, especially on immigration and the economy, and saw Harris' positions as less clear. Regarding unprecedented steps Trump might take, views were mixed. Finally, a large majority said Trump should address all Americans' concerns and work with the opposing party in Congress.
