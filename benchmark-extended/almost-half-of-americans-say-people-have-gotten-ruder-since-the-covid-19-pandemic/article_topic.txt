The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 9,609 U.S. adults conducted from Nov. 12 - 17, 2024, reveals that nearly half (47%) of Americans think public behavior is ruder than before the COVID - 19 pandemic, with a third regularly seeing rude behavior in public. It also explores various public behaviors Americans find unacceptable, such as smoking around others (77% disapproval) and taking photos without permission (74% disapproval). There are demographic differences in views, with older adults, women, and higher - income adults more likely to disapprove of certain behaviors. Despite the perceived increase in rudeness, 84% of adults say it’s easy for them to know what’s appropriate in public, and this ease varies by factors like income and age.
