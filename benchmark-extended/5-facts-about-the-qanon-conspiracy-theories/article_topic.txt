This article presents five facts about Americans' knowledge and views of the QAnon conspiracy theories based on Pew Research Center surveys and analysis. It reveals that awareness of QAnon increased from early to late 2020, with Democrats more aware than Republicans. Those with high political knowledge in both parties are more likely to have heard of it. A majority of those aware think it's bad for the country, with Democrats more likely to hold this view than Republicans. When describing QAnon, people most often mentioned it as a group or conspiracy theory. A YouTube content study showed that QAnon mentions were initially concentrated in a few channels, and some channels heavily featured it in 2020 before several were terminated.
