This article presents nine facts about Americans' views and experiences with marijuana, sourced from Pew Research Center surveys and other organizations. Around nine - in - ten Americans in a 2024 survey support marijuana legalization for medical or recreational use, and views vary by age, political party, and race/ethnicity. Support has grown significantly in the last two decades. Half of U.S. adults say they've used marijuana in their lifetime, with far fewer being current users. Opinions on the impacts of legalizing recreational marijuana, such as economic and criminal justice effects, are diverse, and Democrats and younger adults are more likely to see positive impacts. Most Americans support easing penalties for marijuana convictions, especially younger adults, Democrats, and Black Americans. As of 2024, many states have legalized marijuana for medical and/or recreational use, over half of Americans live in states where both are legal, and about eight - in - ten live in a county with a cannabis dispensary.
