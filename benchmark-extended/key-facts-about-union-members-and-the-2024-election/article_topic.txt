This article focuses on key facts about union members and the 2024 election. Union members, long a core Democratic constituency, are now being courted by both parties. Around 59% of union - affiliated voters identify with or lean toward the Democrats, while 39% associate with the GOP. Currently, half of union voters would support Harris and 43% Trump. Union membership has declined over four decades, from 20.1% in 1983 to 10.0% in 2023, with higher rates in the public sector and in the Northeast, Alaska, and Hawaii. Majorities of Americans think the decline in union membership is bad for the country and working people, with Democrats more likely to hold this view. Voters, especially union members, are more likely to say Harris’ policies would be better for union members. A majority of Americans believe labor unions have a positive effect on the country, though there are large partisan and ideological differences in this view, and a higher share of union members than non - members see unions' positive impact.
