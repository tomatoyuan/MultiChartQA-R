The article, titled “5 facts about how Americans use Facebook, two decades after its launch,” reveals key insights about Americans’ use of Facebook 20 years after its launch. Based on Pew Research Center surveys, around 68% of U.S. adults use Facebook, a share that has been relatively stable since 2016, with only YouTube having a higher adult usage rate. Women and bachelor’s degree holders are more likely to use it. Three - in - ten Americans regularly get news from Facebook, with female, older, and politically evenly - divided users being prominent in this group. A third of U.S. teens use Facebook, a sharp drop from 2014 - 15, and today’s teens prefer platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, and Instagram. Among teens, those from lower - income households, girls, older teens, and those in rural areas are more likely to use Facebook.
