The article, based on a Pew Research Center survey of 8,709 U.S. adults in April 2024, explores Americans' views on the American dream, which is the idea that anyone can achieve success in the U.S. through hard work and determination. It reveals that 53% of Americans think the dream is still possible, 41% believe it was once achievable but not anymore, and 6% say it was never possible. There are differences in these views by age, income, race, ethnicity, partisanship, and education, with older and wealthier adults more likely to think it's still possible. Within each political party, age and income differences in views also persist. Additionally, Americans are divided on whether they can personally achieve the dream: 31% say they've achieved it, 36% are on their way, and 30% think it's out of reach, with variations by race, ethnicity, party, income, and age.
