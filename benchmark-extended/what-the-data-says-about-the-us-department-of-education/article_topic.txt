This article from Pew Research Center delves into the U.S. Department of Education, given its uncertain future under the Trump administration. It notes long - standing conservative calls for its elimination, with Trump reviving these efforts as the department has already cut nearly half its staff and canceled research contracts. The piece answers common questions about the department, including its functions (distributing grants and loans, enforcing civil rights), the number of employees (4,209 as of last September), annual spending (net cost of $218.4 billion in fiscal 2024), types of grants (for K - 12 and higher education), the federal role in K - 12 funding (about 13.6% nationally in fiscal 2022), the states and cities most reliant on federal K - 12 aid (Mississippi, Detroit), the number of college students receiving aid (around 10 million in recent years), the amount of student loan debt owed to the department ($1.47 trillion at the end of fiscal 2024), public opinion (44% favorable, 45% unfavorable in 2024 with a partisan split), and how education administration worked before the current department was created in 1979.
