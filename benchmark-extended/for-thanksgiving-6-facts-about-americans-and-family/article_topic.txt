This article, in anticipation of Thanksgiving on November 23 in the United States, presents six facts about Americans and family togetherness based on Pew Research Center surveys. It reveals that spending time with family is a top personal priority for most Americans across political affiliations, and most U.S. adults value living close to family, with differences by gender, age, education, income, race/ethnicity, and community type. Over half of Americans live within an hour’s drive of some extended - family members. Many pet owners consider their pets part of the family, and a majority of Americans believe close friendships are highly important for a fulfilling life. Additionally, most Americans find political conversations with those they disagree with stressful and frustrating, and thinking about politics often makes them feel exhausted and angry, making it a potentially fraught topic for Thanksgiving gatherings.
