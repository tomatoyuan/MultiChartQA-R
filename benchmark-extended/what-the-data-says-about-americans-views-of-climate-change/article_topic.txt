This article presents findings from Pew Research Center surveys on Americans' views of climate change and related policies. A majority of Americans support prioritizing renewable energy development and U.S. participation in international climate efforts, and believe large businesses, state officials, and the energy industry are doing too little to address climate change. However, views are more divided on phasing out fossil fuels and government incentives for different energy sources. Partisan divides are evident, with Democrats more concerned about climate change, seeing it as a major threat and a higher priority than Republicans. Additionally, perceptions of local climate impacts vary by political affiliation, and there's little consensus on how U.S. efforts compare to other large economies.
